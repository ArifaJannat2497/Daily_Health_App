# Daily Balance Check

A free, bilingual (English / বাংলা) self-check for university students and young adults. Enter a normal day to get:

- **BMI** using the WHO cut-offs for Asian populations, and a healthy weight range for your height
- **Personal targets** for bedtime, brisk walking, screen time for fun, water, fruit and vegetables, and the time of your last meal
- **What matters most for you**, ranked with the ordered-logit model from a study of 912 Bangladeshi university students
- **A 4-week plan** with weekly steps you can tick off

Everything runs in the browser. Answers are saved only on the visitor's own device and are never sent anywhere.

**Live app:** https://arifajannat2497.github.io/daily_health_app/
